Where are my records kept?

On your iPhone and, if you're signed in to iCloud, in your own private iCloud, so they sync to your other devices. Kept has no server and no account to sign in to.

How do I restore a backup?

Kept saves a backup each day you use it and keeps the last 14. Open Settings (the gear on the Garage screen), tap a backup under Backups, and confirm. Your current records are saved first, so a restore can always be undone.

Can I move my history from another app?

Yes. Export your history from the other app as a spreadsheet (CSV), then in Kept open Settings › Import from File. Kept recognizes common column names like Date, Mileage, Description and Cost. Importing the same file twice won't create duplicates.

How do I get Kept Pro on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Settings in Kept and tap Restore Purchases.

How do I cancel the yearly plan?

On your iPhone, go to Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Kept Pro, and tap Cancel. Pro stays active until the end of the period you've paid for. Reminders, backups, import and export stay free.

Why didn't a mileage reminder arrive?

Your phone can't read your car's odometer, so Kept estimates when you'll reach the mileage from your past readings. Update the odometer now and then (there's an optional monthly nudge in Settings), and make sure notifications are allowed for Kept in your iPhone's Settings.
