Privacy Policy

Kept: Car Maintenance Tracker · Effective October 31, 2026

The short version

Kept doesn't collect any information about you. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your records stay on your devices and in your own iCloud, where the developer can't see them.

What the app stores, and where

The vehicles, service records, reminders and receipt photos you enter are saved on your iPhone. If you're signed in to iCloud, they also sync through your own private iCloud database, run by Apple, so they appear on your other devices. That database belongs to your Apple Account, and the developer has no access to it.

Kept also saves a backup each day you use it, in the app's folder on your iPhone (Files app › On My iPhone › Kept). Files you choose to export, such as a spreadsheet, a full backup or a service history PDF, go only where you send them.

Camera and photos

Kept uses the camera only when you tap Take Photo to photograph a receipt. When you choose from your photo library, Kept receives only the photos you pick. These photos are stored with your records as described above.

Notifications

Maintenance reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. No information is sent to a server to deliver them.

Purchases

Kept Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment, and the developer never receives your payment details. Kept checks with the App Store only whether Pro is active.

Children

Kept isn't directed at children under 13, and it doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

You can delete any record or vehicle in the app. Deleting the app removes the data on that iPhone. To remove the iCloud copy, go to Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Account Storage › Kept on your iPhone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
